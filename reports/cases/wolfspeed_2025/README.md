| Old equity | 5% of new equity | $55m at mid value | 0 |

Absolute priority at the plan's own enterprise value would have left the unsecured classes with almost nothing, because the secured claim was allowed including its make-whole and the priority claims (including $549.5m of government grants) rank ahead. The plan instead paid trade creditors in full, gave the notes and Renesas 25-30%, and gave old shareholders 5% of the new equity. The difference is what negotiated restructurings add: value from outside enterprise value (cash on hand, a backstopped rights offering), concessions by the secured class (86% of an allowed claim that included the make-whole), claims left unimpaired to keep the business running, and a gift to equity in exchange for a fast, consensual case.
